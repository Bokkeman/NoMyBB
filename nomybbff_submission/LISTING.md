## Add-on URL slug

nomybb

If that slug is taken, use `nomybb-headlines`.

## Summary

Cleans clickbait headlines on MyBroadband, Newsday, and BusinessTech with your own Deepseek key. Headlines, a short excerpt, and follow-up questions are sent only to api.deepseek.com.

## Description

NoMyBB cleans headlines on MyBroadband, Newsday, and BusinessTech. It drops leads such as "Good news for" and replaces a vague description with the specific place, person, or company named in the article.

You supply your own Deepseek API key. The key stays in this browser's extension storage and is sent only to api.deepseek.com. Firefox will ask you to allow website content and authentication information before the add-on can do that. Headlines that have already been rewritten are reused from a local cache.

On an article page, NoMyBB shows the rewritten headline and a short summary. You can ask one follow-up question about that article. The lists of other headlines under and beside the article are rewritten too. Links inside the article text are left as written.

Topics you list are hidden on the news listing and in those headline lists. Opening an ignored article still shows the cleaned headline and the summary.

You can add another https news site from the settings page. NoMyBB asks Firefox for permission for that site only, and only after you choose Automatic, click Inspect, or save the site.

This Firefox edition keeps its own settings and cache. It does not share them with the Chrome edition.

NoMyBB is not affiliated with MyBroadband, Newsday, BusinessTech, or Deepseek.

A rewritten headline shows the original wording in the browser's normal tooltip when the two differ.
