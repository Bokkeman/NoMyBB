## Name

NoMyBB

## Short description

Cleans clickbait headlines on MyBroadband, Newsday, and BusinessTech with your own Deepseek key.

## Single purpose

NoMyBB rewrites news headlines on sites the user chooses, starting with MyBroadband, Newsday, and BusinessTech. It uses the user's Deepseek API key to replace clickbait and vague wording, show a short summary, answer a follow-up, and hide topics the user lists.

## Detailed description

NoMyBB cleans headlines on MyBroadband, Newsday, and BusinessTech. It drops leads such as "Good news for" and replaces a vague description with the specific place, person, or company named in the article.

You supply your own Deepseek API key. The key stays in this browser's extension storage and is sent only to api.deepseek.com. Headlines that have already been rewritten are reused from a local cache.

On an article page, NoMyBB shows the rewritten headline and a short summary. You can ask one follow-up question about that article. The lists of other headlines under and beside the article are rewritten too. Links inside the article text are left as written.

Topics you list are hidden on the news listing and in those headline lists. Opening an ignored article still shows the cleaned headline and the summary.

You can add another https news site from the settings page. NoMyBB asks the browser for permission for that site only, and only after you choose Automatic, click Inspect, or save the site.

NoMyBB is not affiliated with MyBroadband, Newsday, BusinessTech, or Deepseek.

A rewritten headline shows the original wording in the browser's normal tooltip when the two differ.
